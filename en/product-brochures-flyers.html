Questions

FAQ: Brochures & Flyers

Short answers to what clients ask most before ordering Brochures & Flyers.

How much does brochures & flyers printing cost in Dammam?

The price of brochures & flyers depends on quantity, size, paper and finish. Send us the details on WhatsApp or through the order form on this page, and we'll send a clear quote in Saudi riyals with a delivery date.

What is the difference between a brochure and a flyer?

A flyer is a single sheet printed on one or both sides for quick offers and announcements. A brochure is folded in two or three and carries more information about your services.

What size and paper suit a tri-fold brochure?

The most popular size is A4 folded into three panels, on 150 or 200 gsm coated paper, with optional gloss or matte lamination.

Do you print flyers in large quantities for campaigns?

Yes. We print small and large runs, and recommend digital or offset printing based on quantity to get you the best price.

What file format is best for printing?

A high-resolution PDF or AI file in CMYK with a 3 mm bleed on each side is preferred. If you don't have one, we'll prepare the file for you.

Where is Loon Al-Harf? Do you serve other cities in Saudi Arabia?

Loon Al-Harf is in Dammam, in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, and serves clients in every city across the Kingdom, including Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Al Khobar and Dhahran. Send your order on WhatsApp or by phone, and we'll arrange pickup from the print shop or shipping to your city.
